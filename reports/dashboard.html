Accounting quality and stock returns: S&P 500, 2012-2026

Do firms with strong accounting quality earn higher stock returns? Four classic scores (Piotroski F, Altman Z, Beneish M, Sloan accruals) are computed from SEC EDGAR filings as first reported, and stocks are sorted into quintiles at each month end using only 10-Ks filed at least one trading day earlier (2012-02 to 2026-09).

Read with care. The universe is today's S&P 500 (ex financials and utilities) applied back to 2012, so firms that failed or left the index are missing. That survivorship bias favours the low-quality buckets, and it is the most likely reason the F-score spread comes out negative. The full write-up, with robustness checks and caveats, is in reports/results.md. Chart titles state what the data show; they are computed, not written by hand.

Radar firms (FY2025): the 10 largest by market cap, then the 10 highest M-scores: NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, AVGO, WMT, LLY, JNJ, COST, STX, BG, MRVL, AMCR, CVNA, SNPS, GEN, FCX, CSGP, SOLV. Timeline firm: NVDA, the firm flagged by Beneish in the most fiscal years.
